What WAVE is

WAVE stands for Wray Analysis & Valuation Engine. It is an independent research firm, founded in Singapore in November 2026, that studies whether publicly available data can be used to select US equities with a measurable edge.

The method

Most apparent market-beating strategies are the product of chance or flawed testing. WAVE's process is built to guard against both.

  • Hypothesis firstEach hypothesis and its pass criterion are recorded before any test is run.
  • Point-in-time dataTests use the index membership, prices and reports as they stood on each date.
  • Out-of-sample testingPart of the history is held back and used once.
  • Chance is measuredEvery result is compared with random and randomised-data benchmarks.
  • No fitting to the pastRules are not adjusted to improve a historical result.
  • Rules decideEvery rating and every order follows from fixed, documented calculations. There is no discretionary stock selection.
  • No leverageThe live programme is long-only and uses no borrowed money or short selling.
  • Negative results are keptFailed hypotheses are documented and remain on the record.

What is built

A complete, automated system that runs from public data through to paper trading orders.

Data pipeline

Daily collection from regulatory and public sources: market positioning, prices, company filings, insider transactions and economic releases.

Gloria, the rating model

Rates approximately 500 large US companies from 0 to 100 each day and constructs a diversified portfolio under fixed rules. The methodology is confidential.

Private dashboard

Displays every rating, every order with its rationale, risk measures and a daily comparison with the S&P 500.

Paper trading account

Paper trading at a US broker since 17 November 2026. Orders are generated and placed automatically each week, and every order is logged.

Research notes

Two notes to date. Both report negative results, the usual outcome in quantitative research.

Note 1. Do economic conditions predict relative equity returns?

Equities move strongly with interest rates, credit conditions and oil within the same month. However, none of 17 pre-registered predictions held: by the time the data is published, prices have already adjusted.

Note 2. What does an unrestricted search find?

The strongest of 5,544 candidate relationships appeared statistically significant, yet randomised data produced results of equal strength. All five candidates carried forward failed out of sample.
